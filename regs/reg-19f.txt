Regulation 19F — Regulator’s notices: local land charges
Building Regulations 2010 (SI 2010/2214), current version as served by legislation.gov.uk, fetched 2026-10-04
PART 4 — Supervision of Building Work Otherwise than by Local Authorities
Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/regulation/19F
XML metadata: in force from 2023-10-01

The functions of local authorities under the Local Land Charges Act 1975, as they relate to building work for which the regulator is the building control authority by reason of a regulator’s notice under section 91ZB of the Building Act 1984, are prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of “relevant function” in paragraph 2(5) of Schedule 3 to the Building Safety Act 2022.
