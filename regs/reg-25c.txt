Regulation 25C — New buildings: minimum energy performance requirements
Building Regulations 2010 (SI 2010/2214), current version as served by legislation.gov.uk, fetched 2026-10-04
PART 6 — Energy Efficiency Requirements
Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/regulation/25C
XML metadata: in force from 2022-11-23; extent E and W

Minimum energy performance requirements must be approved by the Welsh Ministers, calculated and expressed in accordance with the methodology approved pursuant to regulation 24, for—
(a) new buildings ..., in the form of target primary energy ... rates; and
(b) when a dwelling is erected, in the form of target fabric performance values.
