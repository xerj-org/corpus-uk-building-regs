Regulation 47E — Compliance and stop notices: withdrawal
Building Regulations 2010 (SI 2010/2214), current version as served by legislation.gov.uk, fetched 2026-10-04
PART 10 — Miscellaneous
Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/regulation/47E
XML metadata: in force from 2023-10-01

1. A building control authority may withdraw a compliance notice or stop notice.
2. Where a building control authority withdraws a compliance notice or stop notice it must as soon as reasonably practicable give the recipient a notice in writing to that effect.
