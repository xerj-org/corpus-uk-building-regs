Regulation 47H — Application to the First-tier Tribunal for a direction in relation to a stop notice
Building Regulations 2010 (SI 2010/2214), current version as served by legislation.gov.uk, fetched 2026-10-04
PART 10 — Miscellaneous
Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/regulation/47H
XML metadata: in force from 2023-10-01

In deciding the question set out in an application to the First-tier Tribunal under section 39A(6) of the Act in relation to a stop notice, the First-tier Tribunal must consider whether it is fair and proportionate, having regard to any representations made, to give the direction.
