Regulation 25 — Minimum energy performance requirements for new buildings
Building Regulations 2010 (SI 2010/2214), current version as served by legislation.gov.uk, fetched 2026-10-04
PART 6 — Energy Efficiency Requirements
Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/regulation/england
This regulation has a different Wales version; it is appended below (the inline text above is the England version).
XML metadata: in force from 2023-06-05; extent E

Minimum energy performance requirements shall be approved by the Secretary of State, calculated and expressed in accordance with the methodology approved pursuant to regulation 24, for—
(a) new buildings (which shall include new dwellings), in the form of target CO2 emission rates; ...
(b) new dwellings, in the form of target fabric energy efficiency rates; and
(c) new buildings in the form of target primary energy rates.

--- ALTERNATIVE VERSION — applies in Wales only (XML Version container "v10005", RestrictExtent W) ---

The Welsh Ministers shall approve minimum energy performance requirements for new buildings, in the form of target CO² emission rates, which shall be calculated and expressed in accordance with the methodology approved pursuant to regulation 24.
