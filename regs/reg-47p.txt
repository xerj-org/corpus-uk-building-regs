Regulation 47P — Application to the magistrates’ court for a direction in relation to a stop notice: Wales
Building Regulations 2010 (SI 2010/2214), current version as served by legislation.gov.uk, fetched 2026-10-04
PART 10 — Miscellaneous
Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2214/regulation/47P
XML metadata: in force from 2026-07-20

1. In deciding the question set out in an application to the magistrates’ court under section 39A(6)(a) of the Act in relation to a stop notice, the magistrates’ court must consider whether it is fair and proportionate, having regard to any representations made, to give the direction.
